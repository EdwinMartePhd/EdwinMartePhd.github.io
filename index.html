Biography Herbert Wertheim College of Engineering

Dr. Edwin Marte Zorrilla is an Instructional Assistant Professor and Graduate Coordinator in the Department of Engineering Education at the University of Florida. He holds a Ph.D. in Engineering Education and an M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Florida, bringing over 15 years of higher education teaching experience across both the United States and the Dominican Republic.

Dr. Marte Zorrilla's research sits at the intersection of educational technology, physiological and emotional learning metrics, artificial intelligence in education, and culturally responsive pedagogy. His work investigates how multimodal sensing (such as heart rate variability and electrodermal activity) and natural language processing can be leveraged to understand student stress, affective fluctuations, and cognitive load during demanding engineering evaluations.

As an educator, Dr. Marte Zorrilla is dedicated to creating experiential, inclusive learning environments. His teaching repertoire includes foundational programming in MATLAB, embedded systems, microcontrollers, CAD, digital design, and engineering design frameworks. He actively mentors robotics teams and leads efforts in integrating emerging AI technologies—such as Intelligent Tutoring Systems—into STEM instruction.
